## 🌟 What is Routine Reader?

Routine Reader is an AI-powered daily activity tracking platform that helps you log, visualize, and improve your daily routine.

Unlike basic habit trackers, Routine Reader analyzes how you actually spend your time and generates personalized suggestions — telling you when you've overworked, skipped meals, or haven't slept enough.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📝 Activity Logging
- Log daily activities with name, category, duration, and time of day
- Categories: Work, Exercise, Sleep, Meals, Learning, Leisure
- Edit and delete activities on the fly

### 📊 Daily Review & Stats
- Summary dashboard showing total time spent per category
- Visual progress bars comparing your day vs a healthy recommended balance
- Instant overview of how your day was spent

### 🤖 AI-Powered Suggestions
- Smart, real-time suggestions based on your logged activities
- Examples:
  - *"You've spent 6 hours on work — consider a short break or exercise"*
  - *"No sleep logged yet — make sure to get 7–8 hours"*
  - *"You haven't logged any meals today"*
- Suggestions update dynamically as you add activities

### 📆 Weekly Overview
- 7-day activity pattern view
- Highlights days with good balance vs off-track days
- Spot trends and improve week over week
